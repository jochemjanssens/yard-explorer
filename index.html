        The full standings

        Squadrats shows only your biggest Yard and Yardinho. Here you get all of them, ranked by size with the gap to the leader.

        02

        Your top 10 on the map

        Each of your ten biggest gets its own colour and a numbered badge, drawn as outlines so the streets stay readable.

        What to ride next

        The single tiles that grow your yards most, the gaps that would merge two yards, and how to grow your Übersquadrat.

        Step 1

        Sign in at squadrats.com and open your map.

        Step 2

        Download the KML export from the panel on the right of the map.

        Step 3

        Drop it here. After new rides, export again and drop the new file.

        Your data stays with you. Your KML is read in your browser and never uploaded. The browser remembers your last export so the map is ready next time. To show place names, the location of each listed yard is looked up with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (cached in your browser). Not affiliated with Squadrats.
