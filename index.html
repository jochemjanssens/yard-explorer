How do I get my KML? ↓
How to upload

Your Squadrats map in three steps

  1. 1

    Open your Squadrats map

    Sign in at squadrats.com with your usual account and go to your map.

    Open squadrats.com ↗
  2. 2

    Download the KML file

    In the panel on the right of the map, download the KML export. You get a file like squadrats-2026-10-29.kml, usually in your Downloads folder.

After new rides, download a fresh KML and drop it again.

The full standings

Squadrats shows only your biggest Yard and Yardinho. Here you get all of them, ranked, named and with the gap to the leader.

Your top 10 on the map

Each of your ten biggest gets its own colour and a numbered tile, drawn as outlines so the streets stay readable.

Every ride, everywhere

Yards from trips far from home are on the map too, tagged Away in the standings. Click one to jump there.

Your data stays with you. Your KML is read in your browser and never uploaded. The browser remembers your last export so the map is ready next time. To show place names, the location of each listed yard is looked up with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (cached in your browser). Not affiliated with Squadrats.
