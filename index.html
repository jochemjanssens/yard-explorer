    Getting your KML

    1. Sign in at squadrats.com and open your map.
    2. Download the KML export from the panel on the right of the map.
    3. Drop it here. After your next ride, export again and drop the new file to see what changed.
    Your data stays with you. The KML is read in your browser and never uploaded; your browser keeps your last two exports so it can compare them. Route planning sends only the start point and waypoints to the public BRouter server. Not affiliated with Squadrats.
