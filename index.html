    A tool for Squadrats riders

    See every Yard and Yardinho you've built, not just the biggest. See what your last ride added. Plan road-bike loops through tiles you haven't ridden yet.

    01

    The full standings

    Squadrats shows only your biggest Yard and Yardinho. Here you get all of them, ranked by size with the gap to the leader.

    02

    What your last ride added

    Drop your newest export to see the new tiles on the map, and how your Yard, Yardinho and Übersquadrat changed.

    03

    Routes through new tiles

    Pick a start and a distance. You get a loop on road-bike roads that crosses as many new tiles as possible, ready as GPX.

    Step 1

    Sign in at squadrats.com and open your map.

    Step 2

    Download the KML export from the panel on the right of the map.

    Step 3

    Drop it here. After your next ride, do it again to see what changed.

    Your data stays with you. Your KML is read in your browser and never uploaded. The browser keeps your last two exports so it can compare them. Route planning sends only the start point and waypoints to the public BRouter server. Not affiliated with Squadrats.
