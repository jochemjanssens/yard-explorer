Yard Explorer

Privacy policy

Website and Android app · Last updated 13 November 2026

Yard Explorer has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your Squadrats export stays on your device. A few things are looked up online to draw the map and name your yards; this page lists exactly what.

Your Squadrats export (KML)

The file you open is read and analysed on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

  • Website: your browser keeps your last export (local storage) so the map is ready next time.
  • Android app: the app keeps your last export in its private storage on your phone. Android's own backup may include it if you have device backup turned on.

You can remove it any time by clearing the site's data in your browser, or the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Place names

To show names like "Leuven" next to your yards, one point per listed yard (rounded to about 100 m) is looked up:

  • Website: with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (their privacy policy).
  • Android app: with Android's built-in geocoder, which is provided by Google Play services on most phones (Google's privacy policy).

Results are cached on your device so each yard is looked up only once. Nothing else is sent with these lookups.

Map tiles

The map images come from OpenStreetMap. Like any website, their servers receive your IP address and which map area you view (their privacy policy).

Your location (GPS)

Location is only used when you press the location button, to show where you are and which tile you're in. It is never sent anywhere and never stored. On Android you can withdraw the permission at any time in the app's settings.

Children, changes and contact

Yard Explorer is not directed at children. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/jochemjanssens/yard-explorer.
